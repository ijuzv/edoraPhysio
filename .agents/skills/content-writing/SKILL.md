# Eudora Physio Content Writing

Use this skill when writing or improving website copy, landing-page sections, headings, CTAs, service descriptions, FAQs, or brand tone.

## Project context

Read `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` before drafting. The audience includes Bangalore residents seeking pain relief, injury recovery, mobility support, home visits, or online physiotherapy. The voice should be calm, empathetic, clear, professional, and evidence-informed - never exaggerated or impersonal.

## Writing guidance

- Start with the visitor’s practical need, then explain the service and the next action in plain language.
- Preserve the approved brand identity: **Eudora Physio** and **The Light After Recovery**. Treat legacy “Eudora Movement House” references as unresolved until explicitly confirmed.
- Use one H1 per page, descriptive H2 sections, short paragraphs, concrete booking CTAs, and scannable service lists.
- Write for both home visits and online consultations where relevant. Clearly describe the video-consultation process and disclose that a full physical exam and certain hands-on tests cannot be done remotely.
- Keep qualifications, service areas, hours, contacts, and clinical statements consistent with `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`.
- Use condition-led service language that reflects patient search intent, then explain the treatment approach without promising a cure or outcome.
- Keep FAQ answers factual, complete, and roughly two to three sentences; they must stand alone and be suitable for visible publication.

## Content boundaries

- Do not fabricate testimonials, reviews, statistics, treatment outcomes, patient counts, credentials, or referrals.
- Do not alter medical, consent, privacy, or legal language without explicit approval; flag uncertainty rather than presenting it as settled fact.
- Mark missing approvals or decisions clearly instead of filling gaps with assumptions.
